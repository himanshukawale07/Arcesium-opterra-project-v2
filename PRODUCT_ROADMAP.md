# Product Roadmap\n\nRelease 0: interactive portfolio demo with master data, pricing, integration preview, quality checks, analytics, reporting, lineage and exports.\n\nRelease 1: persistent exception assignment, comments, approvals, audit history, configurable rules and upload mapping.\n\nRelease 2: authenticated APIs, vendor adaptors, schema registry, feed scheduling, retry/dead-letter handling and Snowflake connectivity.\n\nRelease 3: effective-dated golden records, asset-class pricing, corporate action lifecycle, reconciliation, role controls, historical data services and scalable processing.\n\nPrioritise by business value, risk reduction, user impact, effort, dependencies and control criticality.
