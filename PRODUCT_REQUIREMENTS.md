# Product Requirements\n\nVision: governed and configurable capture, cleansing, organisation and distribution of trusted investment data.\n\nPersonas: Reference Data Analyst, Pricing Analyst, Investment Operations Analyst, Asset Servicing Analyst, Data Operations Manager, Portfolio Manager and Technology Integration.\n\nMVP: security/issuer master; prices, positions and corporate actions; source registry; pricing hierarchies; data-quality rules; exception queue; analytics; document templates; lineage and controlled exports.\n\nBusiness rules: every mastered record has a stable key; prices include source, timestamp, currency and positive value; stale prices are ineligible under configured freshness; no eligible price creates an exception; downstream publication follows validation.\n\nNon-goals: trade execution, official NAV, investment advice, live vendor feeds, actual Snowflake sharing, production APIs or enterprise throughput.\n\nSuccess measures: identifier completeness, price freshness, position reconciliation, exception ageing, manual investigation time and lineage coverage.
