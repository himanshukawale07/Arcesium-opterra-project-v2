# Data Dictionary\n\n- securities.csv: security ID, name, ticker, asset class, ISIN/CUSIP/SEDOL, issuer, currency, exchange, universe.\n- prices.csv: observation ID, security, ticker, vendor, price, currency, timestamp.\n- positions.csv: portfolio, security, ticker, quantity, price, market value, date.\n- corporate_actions.csv: event, security, event type, announcement/ex/record/payment dates, terms, status.\n- transactions.csv: trade ID, date, ticker, asset class, side, quantity, price, currency, status.\n- counterparties.csv: ID, name, type, LEI, country, status.\n- sources.csv: provider, domain, integration type, frequency, status.\n- quality_rules.csv: rule ID, name, domain, severity, description, owner.\n\nAll records and identifiers are fabricated for demonstration and are not authoritative.
